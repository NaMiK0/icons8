What people mean by free icons

Search wording varies, the need does not. The most common variants are “free icons”, “free icon”, “icon free”, “icons free” and “free icons download”.

Formats and where they fit

These searches mention SVG, PNG. Vector formats stay sharp at any size and are the safer default for interfaces; raster formats are simpler to drop into a fixed layout.

Where these assets are used

Most requests for free icons come from interface, presentation and web work, where a consistent set matters more than any single asset.

Before you download

Check the license shown next to an asset before using it, especially in commercial work. Licensing differs between collections, so read it per asset rather than per page.
