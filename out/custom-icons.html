Custom Icons for Your Brand

Standard icon sets often lack the specific details your project requires. Custom icons allow you to define the exact shape, style, and color needed to fit your interface or brand identity.

Define Your Visual Style

Select a style that matches your existing design system, from flat and minimal to 3D or hand-drawn. The choice of style determines how the icon communicates with your users and fits into your layout.

Consider whether your project needs a consistent look across all elements or a unique illustration for a specific feature. Matching the stroke weight and corner radius ensures visual harmony.

Choose the Right Format and Size

Decide between vector formats like SVG for scalable graphics or raster formats like PNG for fixed-size images. Vectors are essential for icons that must appear clearly on high-resolution screens and large displays.

Specify the dimensions required for your application, such as small toolbar buttons or large hero images. The correct file size and resolution prevent pixelation and ensure fast loading times.

Tailor Details to Your Brand

Adjust colors to align with your brand palette, ensuring the icon stands out or blends in as intended. You can request specific color codes to maintain consistency across your digital products.

Modify internal details to represent your specific product or service accurately. A generic symbol may not convey the right message, but a tailored icon can clarify complex functions instantly.
